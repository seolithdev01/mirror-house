## How to play

Every floor is one loop: **frost every 🪞 beacon mirror, then reach the gold stair door** — before the glass catches you. Two to five minutes, sharable by seed, fully offline.

### The descent — floors, the Dwaar, the Truth-Shard

You start on floor 1 of a ten-floor tier and go down. Clearing a floor turns its exit into the **Stair Door**: walking in holds a beat behind a `descending…` banner, then the next floor arms — the ramp adds Echoes and beacons, braids the maze tighter, and shortens Chhaya's leash. The **floor chip** in the HUD's chip row says where you stand: `L01`…`L09` on the ramp, `L10 · DWAAR I ✦` for the tier's threshold.

Floor 5 has a visitor: **Taal**, the beat-walker — a gold-lantern silhouette lurching in time with the music (wall-clock phase, so he keeps the beat even muted). His touch is a temper, not a kill: +20 anger per touch, 1.5 s between touches, and he never takes the floor from you.

Floor 10 is the **Dwaar** — Chhaya's threshold hall, a fixed hand-authored 13×13 labyrinth instead of a procedural floor. Clear it and you claim **✦ Truth-Shard I**: a brief golden banner (`✦ Truth-Shard I claimed — the house deepens…`) and the descent continues — and from floor 11 the house is dressed for Tier 2. Your position in the house is saved; close the tab mid-tier and you resume on the floor you left.

### Tier 2 — the Hall of Ancestors (floors 11–20)

Below the Dwaar the palace changes register. The halls go warm and dark — amber glass, brown stone, deep-teal trim — the music's melody walker moves to a Durga pentatonic, and the floor chip reads `L11 · T2` … `L19 · T2`. The loop never changes — frost the beacons (four a floor), reach the stair door — but the house deepens around it:

- **Taal is the Sentinel now.** The floor-5 cameo only made the house angrier; from floor 11 his touch **catches**, failing the floor like any Echo. He walks every tier-2 floor on the music's beat — surging on it, nearly still between beats, on a two-second loop you can read in his lantern: bright he strides, dim he crawls. **Cross his corridor on the dim beat.** Frost cannot blind him (he walks the floor, not the glass); the beat is the only telegraph you get.
- **The ancestors hang in the glass.** Two or three dim portrait silhouettes wall-mount on every tier-2 floor. They are mirrors like any other — the Echoes see you through their clear panes, and one breath of the Frost Lantern fogs their eyes shut for 25 seconds. Fog them like any glass you would rather they did not watch you through.
- The ramp tightens: five to six Echoes, braids drawn closer, Chhaya 4.5 s behind and tightening to 4.1 s by floor 19.
- **Floor 20 is Dwaar II** — Taal's threshold hall, a second hand-authored 13×13 layout, broader and more open than Dwaar I (96 open cells — the Sentinel needs room to walk his beat). Clear it to claim **✦ Truth-Shard II** — and since the house keeps opening below, the claim shows Dwaar I's brief banner and the descent continues; every full screen now lives deeper, ending at Dwaar IV's act close.

### Tier 3 — the Reflecting Gardens (floors 21–30)

Below Dwaar II the palace turns green: moss walls, water-teal glass, ivory light, hedges for partitions — and the music changes voice with the scenery, a flute leading the pastoral melody from the first note of the tier. The floor chip reads `L21 · T3` … `L29 · T3`. The loop never changes — frost the beacons, reach the stair door — but the house deepens around it:

- **Both Sentinels walk the Gardens.** The ladder accumulates: Taal keeps the beat he learned in the Hall (cross him on the dim beat as ever), and **Gher the Encircler** joins at floor 21. Gher does not chase and cannot be blinded — he watches nothing, no glass, no eyes. He reads your commit and glides at one steady pace to seal the road about three cells ahead of you. The counter is the **feint**: commit one way until he turns for it, then break the commit and take the other way — the moment your heading leaves the path, his prediction dies and he holds while you walk.
- **Water-mirrors.** Two to four still pools stand on every Gardens floor — flat teal cells that are Echo eyes like any clear glass: the hollow ones can see you across the hall through them. You cannot fog water; your one verb is the **ripple** — walk within arm's reach and the surface breaks, its eye closing for ten seconds (the caption says so; its dot on the overhead map dims until the water stills). Cross wide, or ripple first and move while it is blind.
- The ramp tightens: six Echoes on every floor, four beacons, Chhaya 4.2 s behind tightening to 3.7 s (3.4 s in Dwaar III). No portraits down here — the Gardens hang water, not ancestors.
- **Floor 30 is Dwaar III** — the garden hall, the most open threshold in the house: dotted hedge runs (never a solid wall) and two sightlines clear across it, because the water needs the long view. Clear it to claim **✦ Truth-Shard III** — and since the Carnival opened below, the claim takes Dwaar I's brief banner too and the descent continues; the full screen is the act close's now.
- For testers (`?test=1`): the Gardens read straight out of the diagnostics hook — `gherPos()` (the Encircler's live spot), `mirrors().water` (the pool census), and `audioState().scale`/`.voice` (the live scale and lead voice: `[0,2,5,7,9]` + `sine` down here, the tier-one pair on venue floors).

### Tier 4 — the Carnival of Faces (floors 31–40) — ACT I COMPLETE

Below Dwaar III the palace throws a carnival: rose and magenta glass over plum-dark walls, candle-glow diamonds on the floor lattice — and the music turns mischievous, the melody flattening its third and handing the lead back to the reed. The floor chip reads `L31 · T4` … `L39 · T4`. The loop never changes — frost the beacons, reach the stair door — but the house plays its last tricks of the act:

- **All three Sentinels walk the Carnival.** The ladder accumulates one last time: Taal keeps his beat (cross him on the dim beat), Gher keeps reading your heading (feint him) — and **Aavart the Ambusher** joins at floor 31, the fourth and fastest Sentinel, and the one who reads your **pace**. He projects where your stride is committed — up to 1.8 seconds of travel ahead at a full sprint, barely a step if you stand still — and runs at 2.4 m/s to cut the corner before you reach it. Frost cannot blind him (he watches nothing but your stride); the counter is the **pace trick**: walk where you meant to sprint, stop dead mid-corridor — his projection only retargets every 1.2 seconds, so a pace changed inside that window leaves him ambushing an empty road.
- **Distorting mirrors — the carnival glass.** Two or three bent rose panes hang on every floor (the wavy rose squiggle on your overhead map marks each one), and while one can see you it **lies about where you are**: every Echo's mirror-sight — and the map's red pings — read you a cell or two off from your true standing, so the hunt chases the spot the glass shows instead of you. They are eyes like any standing glass: one breath of the Frost Lantern (F) fogs a pane for 25 seconds and the house remembers where you truly stand — fogging can even shake a chase outright, if the Echo was following the lie.
- The ramp tightens one last notch: **five Echoes on every floor** — a deliberate step back from the Gardens' six, the playability call that lets three lethal Sentinels own the stage — four beacons, Chhaya 3.8 s behind tightening to 3.3 s. No portraits, no water down here — the Carnival hangs bent glass.
- **Floor 40 is Dwaar IV** — the funhouse hall: four offset alcoves (one per wall, never compass-aligned) around a central carousel ring you circle while the Sentinels circle you. Clear it to claim **✦ Truth-Shard IV** — **ACT I closes** here, and since Act II opened below, the claim takes the same brief golden banner as the Dwaars above and the stairs descend into the Flood Halls (the full screen now belongs to Dwaar V's tier close).
- For testers (`?test=1`): the Carnival reads straight out of the diagnostics hook — `avartPos()` (the Ambusher's live spot), `mirrors().distort` (the carnival-glass census), `apparentPlayer()` (where the house says you stand right now — the truth, or a pane's displaced lie), and `audioState().scale`/`.voice` (`[0,2,3,7,9]` + `triangle` down here).

### Tier 5 — the Flood Halls (floors 41–50) — ACT II OPENS

Below Dwaar IV the palace drowns: drowned indigo-green walls, teal glass, lantern light gone pale — and the music submerges with the scenery, the melody walking a dark pentatonic (`[0,3,5,7,10]`) on the triangle. The floor chip reads `L41 · T5` … `L49 · T5`. The loop never changes — frost the beacons, reach the stair door — but **ACT II — THE DECEPTION** announces itself here: the first floor-41 build of a session raises a three-second banner (`ACT II — THE DECEPTION — The house has begun copying you.`), and the house plays its new tricks:

- **The house copies you — mirror-you decoys.** Every 45 seconds or so (36–54, jittered) the house plants a glass copy of you at a cell 8–12 cells away — an exact clone of your coat and lantern — and it walks toward the exit at its own steady pace, ignoring you completely. It cannot hurt you, frost does not touch it, and it never drowns. But the Echoes cannot tell a copy from the original: **an Echo chases whichever of you is nearer**, so a copy between you and the hunt is a shield you can spend. Walk into a copy and it shatters — **+5 shards** banked toward the clear, forfeited with any fail like beacon shards; two copies may stand at once, the caption names each one the house sends, and on the overhead map the copy is your arrow drawn hollow while the solid gold arrow is the you that is real.
- **The halls flood.** From your first step the water rises — **3 minutes to 1.2 m deep**, a translucent teal-green plane climbing the walls. Past 0.6 m **everyone wades at 85 % speed** (you, the Echoes, the Sentinels — the copies excepted: they never drown); past 0.9 m **the standing glass sinks far enough that it stops being an eye** — the flood blinds the mirrors for you, no frost spent. The glass visibly drowns as it goes, its panes shrinking toward half height; the caption warns once (`The halls are flooding…`). In **Dwaar V's sunken arena the flood runs on a faster clock — 2 minutes** — so the duel is also a race.
- **The rotation — the house rotates its wardens.** The ladder stops accumulating and starts rotating: **Taal never rests** (his beat walks every floor from 11 down through 50 — cross him on the dim beat as ever; the Wings below give him his first rest), while **Gher and Aavart sit the 41–49 ramp out** and are **called down together for one hall only — the Dwaar V duel**. The ramp hunts you with your shadow and the beat alone; the tier's teeth are the water and the copies. The escalation returns at the threshold: floor 50 is the first hall where **all four Sentinels walk together**.
- The ramp itself eases the Echo count to make room: **four Echoes on every procedural floor** (a step back from the Carnival's five — the flood and the decoys are the teeth now), four beacons, braids drawn tighter to 0.332 by floor 49, Chhaya 3.6 s behind tightening to 3.1 s (2.9 s in Dwaar V — the arena floods, she does not wait).
- **Floor 50 is Dwaar V** — the sunken arena: concentric rings, a two-cell promenade outside a mid ring wall with exactly four gates at the compass midpoints, an inner corridor circling the 3×3 sanctum — 180°-symmetric (the exit mirrors the entrance), fully connected, 101 open cells of room for four wardens. Clear it to claim **✦ Truth-Shard V** — and since the Furnace Wings opened below (t6), the claim takes the same brief golden banner as the Dwaars above (`✦ Truth-Shard V claimed — the house deepens…`) and the stairs descend into the heat; the full screen now belongs to Dwaar VI's tier close.
- For testers (`?test=1`): the Flood Halls read straight out of the diagnostics hook — `tier()` (5), `waterLevel()` (the live depth in meters, `0` off the flood floors), `decoys()` (the live copies — the e2e uses the `forceDecoy()` dev faucet to skip the 45 s clock), and `audioState().scale`/`.voice` (`[0,3,5,7,10]` + `triangle` down here).

### Tier 6 — the Furnace Wings (floors 51–60) — ACT II DEEPENS

Below Dwaar V the palace catches fire: ember and copper glass over charred charcoal walls, a low furnace glow breathing near the floor, a faint warm shimmer in the air — and the music turns urgent, the melody walking a Phrygian-adjacent pentatonic (`[0,1,5,7,8]`) on the triangle. The floor chip reads `L51 · T6` … `L59 · T6`. The loop never changes — frost the beacons, reach the stair door — but the Wings burn, and the house plays its hottest tricks:

- **The heat.** The furnace halves your frost and doubles your cracks. A fogged pane **evaporates in half the time — 12.5 seconds, not 25** — so every blind corridor you cut is a door that closes and reopens while you watch; and every mirror you sprint into **cracks 25 anger deep instead of 15** — four careless cracks to collapse, not seven. The air itself shimmers (a static warm veil, safe under Reduce flashing), and Aaina will sell you the warning when it matters: _the furnace breathes — your frost melts fast, and every crack you leave burns wider._ Frost early, frost wide, and arrive at a walk more than ever.
- **The rotation turns.** The wardens trade places: **the Furnace Wings are Gher and Aavart's home** — the Encircler and the Ambusher both walk every floor 51–60, fresh off their Dwaar V recall — while **Taal takes his first rest ever** (51–59: the beat-walker who walked every floor from 11 down sits the heat out — his beat-and-water pairing was the Flood's gimmick, and the heat re-tunes this fight). Chhaya, as always, rests never. And at the threshold the house called everyone down: **Dwaar VI is the second all-four-Sentinel hall**. (Tier 7 lengthened Taal's rest through floor 65 — the crucible now replays as Chhaya + Gher + Aavart, and his call-down moved to the Whisper Library's floor 66.)
- **The warden guards the copies.** Act II's deception deepens: the mirror-you decoys still spawn down here (they walk floors 41–60 — the furnace never drowns them either), but **Gher now reads a live copy's cell as one of his objectives**. A copy crossing his sector pulls the seal straight onto it — he stands over the thing the Echoes are chasing, and farming the +5 shards means breaking the seal yourself. When he first takes the bait, the caption admits it: _The warden guards the copy._ The feint works on the copy's road exactly as it works on yours.
- The ramp itself keeps the Wings walkable: **four Echoes on every procedural floor** (Dwaar VI alone carries five — the crucible duel is the tier's apex; its teeth are the heat and the guarded copies), four beacons, braids drawn tighter to 0.352 by floor 59, Chhaya 3.5 s behind tightening to 2.9 s (2.8 s in Dwaar VI — the crucible burns, she does not wait).
- **Floor 60 is Dwaar VI** — the crucible forge: twin parallel forge corridors (two 3-wide halls running the forge's length — the longest corridors in the house) joined at the ends and joined in the middle by a central 3×3 crucible chamber with exactly four connecting gaps — 180°-symmetric (the exit mirrors the entrance), fully connected, 101 open cells for four wardens and the heat. Clear it to claim **✦ Truth-Shard VI** — and since the Whisper Library opened below (t7), the claim takes the same brief golden banner as the Dwaars above (`✦ Truth-Shard VI claimed — the house deepens…`) and the stairs descend into the stacks; the full screen now belongs to Dwaar VII's tier close.
- For testers (`?test=1`): the Wings read straight out of the diagnostics hook — `tier()` (6), `heat()` (`{ on, fogMs, crackAnger }` — the live heat read-out, `fogMs` 12500 down here), `gherSeal()` (the cell the Encircler has sealed right now — watch it land on a copy), `decoys()`/`forceDecoy()` as ever, and `audioState().scale`/`.voice` (`[0,1,5,7,8]` + `triangle`).

### Tier 7 — the Whisper Library (floors 61–70) — ACT II LISTENS

Below Dwaar VI the palace goes quiet and moonlit: lavender-grey glass over slate walls, dusk-dark floor diamonds, a moon-pale lantern breath — and the music whispers with it, the melody walking a slow hushed set (`[0,2,3,7,8]`) on the sine (the Gardens' flute voice, played under a 0.55 intensity ceiling so it stays sparse even mid-chase). The floor chip reads `L61 · T7` … `L69 · T7`. The loop never changes — frost the beacons, reach the stair door — but the house has learned to listen:

- **The Echoes hear, not see.** Below Dwaar VI the mirrors go dark for the hunt: tier-7 Echoes have **no mirror-sight at all** — clear glass, fogged glass, none of it means anything to them, and the fog-a-crowd confusion trick does not work on them either. They track **noise** instead: **sprinting rings loud (7 m), walking rings soft (4 m), standing still is silent**, and your verbs ping once at the action — **frost 2.5 m, a silvered-door push 2.5 m, a True-Mirror step 2.5 m**. A ring lives about a second; break off, go quiet, and a chase forgets you in two. The whole tier is a pace economy: creep the stacks, sprint only in bursts, and never run past a shelf you cannot see around. When one first hears you, the caption admits it: _A shelf-warden heard you._ — and Aaina will sell you the standing rule: _They are blind here — but they listen. Walk. Never run the stacks._
- **The rotation holds its breath — then the beat comes back.** The Library opens as the quietest floors in the house: **Chhaya and the blind wardens only on 61–65** (Gher keeps the Furnace he has always walked, then rests 61–69; Aavart rests the whole tier and returns in tier 8). At **floor 66 Taal's beat returns** — his rest lengthened from the Furnace's 51–59 clear through 65, and mid-tier the beat walks again: bright he strides, dim he crawls, and every bright accent now rings like a sprint at his position — **noise he ignores but everything with ears chases**, so his corridor is both the loudest danger in the stacks and the best decoy in the house: pull the wardens onto his beat and walk away quiet. (And tier 8 keeps the promise: the Atrium below wakes Aavart — Taal and Gher rest 71–79 and the Dwaar VIII hall at 80 calls all four down.)
- The ramp itself hushes: **four Echoes on every procedural floor** (Dwaar VII alone carries five — the duet is the apex; the tier's teeth are the ears and the noise economy, not the echo count), four beacons, braids drawn tighter to 0.392 by floor 69, Chhaya 3.3 s behind tightening to 2.8 s (2.6 s in Dwaar VII — the stacks hush, she does not wait). The mirror-you copies still spawn down here (they walk everything from 41 on) — and the Echoes still chase the nearer of you and a copy (down here they hunt by ear — the copies walk silent, so it is your footsteps the wardens key on).
- **Floor 70 is Dwaar VII** — the library stacks: four long parallel shelf-walls with fully-open reading aisles between them, and four reading alcoves tucked at the shelf ends (180°-mirrored pairs) — 180°-symmetric (the exit mirrors the entrance), fully connected, 95 open cells of quiet for the **Taal + Gher duet** to walk: the beat and the seal, in a hall where everything listens. Clear it to claim **✦ Truth-Shard VII** — and since the Frozen Atrium opened below (t8), the claim takes the same brief golden banner as the Dwaars above (`✦ Truth-Shard VII claimed — the house deepens…`) and the stairs descend into the ice; the full screen now belongs to Dwaar VIII's tier close.
- For testers (`?test=1`): the Library reads straight out of the diagnostics hook — `tier()` (7), `noise()` (the live noise ring's size — 0 while you stand still, ~24 under a held sprint), `echoMode(i)` (the wardens' witness: `patrol` → `chase` → `patrol` as you ring and hush), `decoys()`/`forceDecoy()` as ever, and `audioState().scale`/`.voice` (`[0,2,3,7,8]` + `sine`, with intensity hushed to ≈ 0.11 on a calm floor).

### Tier 8 — the Frozen Atrium (floors 71–80) — ACT II FREEZES

Below Dwaar VII the palace ices over: frost-white glass on deep void-blue walls, ice-teal trim, a pale lantern breath — and the music goes austere with the scenery, the melody walking a suspended pentatonic (`[0,2,5,7,10]`, no third — the whole tier sounds held) on the **sawtooth**, the cold strings. The floor chip reads `L71 · T8` … `L79 · T8`. The loop never changes — frost the beacons, reach the stair door — but the house turns your oldest tool around:

- **Every pane arrives frozen.** The Atrium's standing mirrors — the hall's glass and the two hero mirrors by the doors alike — spawn **encased in ice**: frost-white, opaque, eye-blind like fogged glass (eight frozen panes on a typical floor). The beacon panes and the True-Mirror pair stay live glass — the capture objective never starts sealed and the step-through is never blind — but every other eye in the house starts shut. You walk in blind-both-ways: the wardens cannot see you, and you cannot see you.
- **Your breath thaws — and thawed glass draws the wardens.** On the ice the Frost Lantern inverts: `F` on a frozen pane **THAWS** it (the caption says so once a floor: _The ice gives way — the glass watches._), and a thawed pane is honest glass again — **a live Echo eye AND a lure**: any warden patrolling within ten meters walks to the thawed pane and stands there watching it. Frost on a thawed pane **cold-fogs** it blind again — ten minutes of dark, the eye shuts, the lure ends — and fog on ice does nothing at all: you cannot fog what you have not woken. A thaw lasts the whole attempt (a failed floor re-freezes the atrium), so every corridor is a choice: **thaw to see and to pull the wardens where you want them — or leave the glass iced and keep the hall blind.** Aaina will sell you the rule when it matters: _The Atrium freezes every pane. Your breath thaws them — and thawed glass draws the wardens. Choose what they watch._
- **The rotation wakes the sleeper.** The Ambusher is back: **Aavart walks every floor 71–79** beside Chhaya — his whole-tier rest ended exactly where the Library promised — and the pace-read he runs is the one sense the ice cannot fool (he watches your stride, not the glass). **Taal and Gher rest 71–79** — the beat and the seal sit the cold out — and the Dwaar VIII hall calls **all four** down: the third all-four-Sentinel crucible, in a hall where every pane starts blind. The Echoes see glass again down here (the hearing was the Library's), but the frozen panes mean there is nothing to see through until you thaw it. (And tier 9 below ends the rotation itself: the Warrens hunt all-four on every floor — no one rests again.)
- The ramp itself keeps the ice walkable: **four Echoes on every procedural floor** (Dwaar VIII alone carries five — the crucible duel is the tier's apex; its teeth are the frozen panes and the thaw-to-lure economy, not the echo count), four beacons, braids drawn tighter to 0.412 by floor 79, Chhaya 3.2 s behind tightening to 2.7 s (2.5 s in Dwaar VIII — the atrium freezes, she does not wait). The mirror-you copies still spawn down here (they walk everything from 41 on).
- **Floor 80 is Dwaar VIII** — the frozen atrium itself: a grand central ring — a wall ring around a fully-open court with exactly four gaps — and four radial galleries running to the corners, 180°-symmetric (the exit mirrors the entrance), fully connected, 97 open cells of ice for the **all-four duel**. Clear it to claim **✦ Truth-Shard VIII** — and since the Warrens opened below (t9), the claim takes the same brief golden banner as the Dwaars above (`✦ Truth-Shard VIII claimed — the house deepens…`) and the stairs descend into the iron; the full screen now belongs to Dwaar IX's tier close.
- For testers (`?test=1`): the Atrium reads straight out of the diagnostics hook — `tier()` (8), `mirrors().frozen`/`.thawed` (the ice census), `mirrorState(id)` (a pane's live state — `frozen`, `clear` once thawed, `fogged` once cold-fogged, `captured`), `los(x1, y1, x2, y2)` (the sight-line read the lure matcher uses), `echoMode(i)` as ever, and `audioState().scale`/`.voice` (`[0,2,5,7,10]` + `sawtooth`).

### Tier 9 — the Warden's Warrens (floors 81–90) — ACT III OPENS

Below Dwaar VIII the palace turns to iron: warden-steel glass over gunmetal floors, iron-red lattice diamonds, gold lines under near-void shadow — and the music goes dread with the scenery, the melody walking the iron pentatonic (`[0,1,3,7,8]` — root, flat second's bite, minor third, fifth, flat sixth) on the sawtooth. The floor chip reads `L81 · T9` … `L89 · T9`. The loop never changes — frost the beacons, reach the stair door — but the first floor-81 build of a session raises a three-second banner (`ACT III — THE GROUND DOOR — The door is real. So is what guards it.`), and the house stops rotating its wardens:

- **All four Sentinels hunt together — on every floor.** Row 9 of the ladder, the apex the whole rotation pointed at: **no one rests any more**. Chhaya replays your path (3.0 s behind at 81, tightening to 2.5 s by 89 — 2.3 s in Dwaar IX), Taal walks his beat (cross him on the dim beat), Gher reads your heading and seals the road ahead (feint him), and Aavart cuts the corner your pace promised (change pace) — all four, every floor 81–90, with five Echoes walking beside them. The counters have not changed; there are just four of them, all at once. The apex is the exam on the whole house, and every answer is one you already know: never retrace, watch the lantern, break the commit, break the stride. **You will not out-fight them. Out-think them, or die in the iron.**
- **Every mastered mechanic returns — thin.** The Warrens stack the old lessons into the new metal: the Furnace Wings' **heat re-lights** (your frost evaporates in 12.5 seconds and every sprint-crack burns 25 anger — four careless cracks to collapse), the Reflecting Gardens' **water pools** stand in the corridors (two or three a floor — walk close and the ripple blinds an eye for ten seconds), and the Carnival's **distorting panes** hang in the iron (one or two — while one sees you, the hunt chases the spot the glass shows, so fog the liar). Nothing is new; everything is stacked.
- The ramp itself is the last tightening: **five Echoes on every procedural floor** (Dwaar IX alone carries six — the apex duel carries one more than every elder duel hall's five; the teeth are the hunt and the texture, not the echo count), four beacons, braids drawn tighter to 0.432 by floor 89, and the mirror-you copies still spawn down here (they walk everything from 41 on).
- **Floor 90 is Dwaar IX** — the Warden's own warren: the tightest hand-authored hall in the house (85 open cells), a waist band with exactly three doors, twelve dead ends and no lane longer than five — small, twisty, personal, 180°-symmetric (the exit mirrors the entrance), fully connected, and walked by **all four**. Clear it to claim **✦ Truth-Shard IX** — and since the Foundations opened below (t10), the claim takes the same brief golden banner as the Dwaars above (`✦ Truth-Shard IX claimed — the house deepens…`) and the stairs descend out of the iron and into the stone; the full screen now belongs to floor 100's Ground Door finale.
- For testers (`?test=1`): the Warrens read straight out of the diagnostics hook — `tier()` (9), `taalPos()`/`gherPos()`/`avartPos()` (all non-null — the apex's witness), `heat()` (`{ on: true, fogMs: 12500, crackAnger: 25 }`), `mirrors().water`/`.distort` (the returned texture census), `decoys()`/`forceDecoy()` as ever, and `audioState().scale`/`.voice` (`[0,1,3,7,8]` + `sawtooth`).

### Tier 10 — the Foundations (floors 91–100) — THE FINALE

Below Dwaar IX the palace gives up its oldest material: **there are no mirrors at all.** Near-black stone floors under pale stone light, gold lattice lines the only ornament, a three-note scale (`[0,5,7]`) walks the melody on the sine — the sparsest music in the game, the house holding its breath — and the floor chip reads `L91 · T10` … `L99 · T10`. The loop never changes — capture the beacons, reach the stair door — but every tool the house ever gave you or took from you is gone: no standing glass, no portraits, no pools, no bent panes, no True Mirrors, no heat, no flood, no copies of you (the house stopped sending them at 90 — a copy is glass, and the Foundations have none). Nothing to fog, nothing to ripple, nothing to read. Just the maze, plain and hard — the tightest tread in the game — and what walks it with you:

- **The hunt ends — the Sentinels rest, all four, for a whole tier.** Chhaya replays your path as always (2.9 s behind at 91, tightening to 2.5 s by 99), six Echoes keep the one crowd the Foundations know — and Taal's beat, Gher's seal and Aavart's ambush all stop at 90. The loneliest floors in the house are yours to walk alone.
- **Floor 100 is the Glazier's Ground — the house's master hunts you himself.** The chip reads `L100 · THE GROUND`, the room is a plain maze (the Glazier built his foundations plainly — he IS the gimmick), the four beacons are the last pedestals — and **he** is there: a tall white figure with a full-white lantern, the only pure-white thing in the house. Every twelve seconds he abandons his pursuit and relocates to the farthest point in the maze from where you stand, then walks straight at you at one steady pace. Frost means nothing to him — there is no glass left to fog. Copies mean nothing — none exist. Water means nothing — the Foundations are dry. He cannot be blinded, lured or slowed; he can only be outlasted between teleports. His touch fails the floor like any catch.
- **The Ground Door.** Capture the four pedestals, reach the gold door at the mirror corner, and the campaign ends the only way it could: **`ACT III COMPLETE — THE GROUND DOOR OPENS`** — one hundred floors and your banked shards counted, Truth-Shard X named in the tally, and the line the whole descent earned: _One hundred floors. One door. You walked out._ ↻ Play again starts a fresh campaign on a new seed; Stay lets you stand in the ending a moment. The house is terminal at 100 — there is no floor 101, and there never will be.
- For testers (`?test=1`): the Foundations read straight out of the diagnostics hook — `tier()` (10), `taalPos()`/`gherPos()`/`avartPos()` (all NULL — the rest pinned), `glazierPos()` (NULL on 91–99, the master's live spot on the Ground), `mirrors()` (total 4 — the pedestals are the only mirror-refs — and every glass census zero), `heat()` (`{ on: false, … }` — the furnace burned out at 90), and `audioState().scale`/`.voice` (`[0,5,7]` + `sine`).

> **THE FINALE.** The 100-floor building from the first blueprint, delivered floor by floor: ten tiers × ten floors, 2026-11-08 → 2026-11-12, every floor playable, every one of them seeded, deterministic and saved. The brief said _"a 100 storied building where each and every floor is a 3D room with well defined maze"_ — this is that building, all hundred storeys of it, and the door at the bottom works.

### Frost — the only attack you get (`F`)

The Frost Lantern breathes on the mirror you face (press `F` or the **❄ Frost** button): the glass fogs over for 25 seconds and its reflection dies. Echoes see you only through clear glass, so a fogged mirror is a door you just closed in their face — fog enough eyes around one Echo and it stalls, confused, slowly spinning. Each breath spends about a third of your lantern's air; it refills in four seconds. (In the Furnace Wings the heat burns a fog off in half the time — 12.5 seconds — so frost wide, not lone. In the Whisper Library the blind wardens cannot be confused by your fog at all — frost there still captures beacons, but the breath itself pings 2.5 m of noise, so frost on the move sparingly. In the Frozen Atrium the breath wakes what it touches: frost on ice THAWS it — a live eye and a warden lure — and frost on thawed glass cold-fogs it for ten minutes; see the tier above. And in the Warden's Warrens below, the furnace re-lights — the heat is back on 81–90, 12.5-second fogs and 25-anger cracks — with all four Sentinels walking at once; see the tier above that. And at the very bottom, in the Foundations, the glass is simply gone: nothing to fog but the four pedestal beacons per floor, no heat to burn them — the lantern's last work is capture, not concealment.)

### Movement — three modes (HUD buttons, top right)

Three fully playable ways to walk — switch anytime with the buttons at the top right. Your choice is remembered between sessions.

| HUD button | Mode             | How it works                                                                                                               |
| ---------- | ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ⌨ Keys     | Keyboard only    | `W`/`S` or `↑`/`↓` walk, `←`/`→` or `Q`/`E` turn, `Shift` sprint. No mouse needed.                                         |
| 🖱 Mouse    | Mouse only       | Hold the **left button** to walk, hold the **right button** to sprint, steer by moving the cursor left or right of center. |
| ⌨+🖱 Both   | Hybrid (default) | `WASD` move and strafe, mouse to look (click the view to capture the pointer), `Q`/`E` also turn, `Shift` sprint.          |

### Accessibility

The house is meant to be survivable by everyone it hunts. The front door is the **⚙ Settings** overlay (next to 🌐): **Display** (text size) · **Access** (reduce flashing, captions) · **Performance** (quality presets) · **Controls** (remap every key) — every change applies at once and is saved on your device.

- **Four ways to see it** — `V` cycles Focus (first person), Quad (all four walls at once), Overhead (a walking 2D map of what you have explored), and `T`'s tactical pause (the floor freezes while you plan; click to mark up to eight waypoints). The map views are first-class play, not minimaps — low-vision players run whole floors from Overhead.
- **One-handed play** — the 🖐 Mouse mode is complete on its own: left button walks toward the cursor, right button sprints. Keyboard-only (⌨ Keys) is equally complete with no mouse at all.
- **Remap every key** — ⚙ → Controls: all 15 actions rebind (click a row, press a key); the conflict guard stops double-binding, arrows/Shift stay hardwired as universal alternates, and Reset returns to defaults. Close hints and prompts always name the live key.
- **Reduce flashing** (⚙ → Access) steadies every pulsing light and fades overlays instead of popping them; **captions** print sound cues as text, bottom-centre; **text size** scales the whole HUD 100/125/150%.
- Every icon button carries a screen-reader label (English and हिंदी), overlays are real dialogs (Tab cycles inside, `Esc` closes), and the page's `lang` follows the 🌐 button.
- **Quality presets** (⚙ → Performance): Auto follows your frame rate; Low/Medium/High are one click, with sharpness applied at once and mirror detail on the next floor.

The full statement — every mode named with the file that implements it, the who's-who entity identity audit (silhouette + motion + caption, never colour alone), and the reduced-flash audit — is [docs/A11Y.md](docs/A11Y.md).

### Views — `V` to cycle, `T` to stop and think

`V` walks **Focus → Quad → Overhead → Focus**. Focus is the first-person view. Quad fills the screen with four live panes — front, left, right, back (the side panes render on alternate frames to hold the frame budget). Overhead is a walking 2D map that shows only what you have explored: corridors fill in as you enter them, and a fading ping marks Chhaya each time she walks. From the Overhead map, `T` enters the **tactical pause**: the floor freezes while you plan — click cells to drop up to eight ivory waypoints, the first `Esc` clears your marks, and the next `Esc` (or any `V`) returns you to your eyes with the route still drawn on the map. The run clock keeps running while you plan: best times are pause-proof by construction.

### House Anger — don't sprint into the glass

Sprinting body-first into a mirror cracks it: +15 anger, once per pane. Seven cracks — 100 anger — and the mirrors collapse: the floor fails and instantly re-arms, and the shards you had banked on it are forfeit. Walking never cracks anything. When glass is near, arrive at a walk. In the Furnace Wings (floors 51–60) the heat makes every crack burn wider — +25 anger, four cracks to collapse — and the Warden's Warrens re-light that furnace (81–90: the same +25, the same four-crack collapse, with all four Sentinels walking) — down there, never sprint near glass at all.

### Chhaya — the shadow behind you

Chhaya walks your exact path, seven seconds behind on floor 1 — and closer with every floor you descend (4.6 s by floor 9, 4 s in the Dwaar). Never retrace your steps — you will meet yourself — and never linger where you have been. A True Mirror hop puts you somewhere your trail never went.

### Passages — `Space` and `E`

- `Space` — **step through a True Mirror** to its far pair, emerging facing away from the glass. The HUD hints `Space — step through` when one is in range; it is a committed step, not a cancelable one.
- `E` — **push a silvered door**: a mirror-finished push-wall that slides aside into hidden rooms and shortcuts (the HUD hints `E — push`). A pushed door stays open for good — the house remembers its own secrets.

### Shards

Scored per cleared floor: 5 per beacon captured, +25 for a flawless floor (nothing cracked), +10 per Echo you confused at least once. Beacon shards bank as _pending_ and are only paid out when you actually clear — get caught or collapse and they are forfeit. Your banked total (💠 chip) lives on your device beside your streak.

### Aaina — the lantern speaks (`I`)

Press `I` (or the 💬 button) to open Aaina's panel — the FAQ's diegetic sister, docked beside the maze. The floor keeps running while she talks. Her **Hint** tab sells knowledge for shards: ◆5 for the first hint on a floor, ◆10 for the second, ◆15 after that (the price resets on a new floor), and only banked shards — the 💠 chip — can pay, never the pending ones riding the current run. She names her subject before you buy (the beacons, the open stair door, the thing hunting you…) and will not repeat herself until the situation changes. Her **Lore** tab unlocks a memory per cleared floor; shards cannot buy those.

### Sound — alive from your first input

The music is generated live in your browser — raga-inspired classical-fusion, fully original, no samples. It stays silent until your first keypress or click (browsers require a gesture), then follows the house: a calm drone while you explore, tabla and melody sharpening as anger and pursuit climb, silence-hugging calm once the floor is cleared. `M` or the 🔊 button mutes; the choice is remembered on your device.

### URL options

- `?seed=CODE` — play a specific campaign. Codes are 1–16 characters from `ABCDEFGHJKMNPQRSTUVWXYZ23456789` (no easily-confused letters). The code is the campaign's base: the same code always builds the same floors, so a code shared is a house shared. (Dev builds also accept `&floor=N` to jump straight to a floor.)
- `?look=coat-glass` — dress your surveyor; you'll meet the result in the mirrors. The separator may be `-` or `.`, unknown parts fall back to the default `indigo-gold`. All 12 combinations are valid:

  | **teal**             | `teal-gold`   | `teal-teal`   | `teal-white`   |

### Timer, Daily, streak, best

The run timer starts on your first input and stops when you reach the stair door. Clearing a floor records your **best time** for that floor — every floor of a base is its own bucket (`BASE:floor`), so floor 3 never competes with floor 7 — and grows your **streak** — clear at least one floor every consecutive day to keep it alive. The **★ Daily** button loads the seed derived from today's date: everyone playing the Daily starts the same hundred-floor spine that day — Act I's whole descent, Act II's four tiers and Act III's full run to the Ground Door.

### Your data stays on your device — and can travel

Everything persistent — your look, input mode, mute, language, shards, streak, best times, and your floor in the campaign — lives in one IndexedDB record inside your own browser. No accounts, no servers, no telemetry; nothing leaves your machine unless you carry it yourself. (A legacy localStorage save from earlier milestones migrates itself once on boot.) The full statement — cookies, the export file's exact contents, the CSP — is [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md); where every byte of code, art, and audio comes from is [PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md); and [PLAYTEST.md](PLAYTEST.md) is the kit for running five strangers through their first floor.

- **💾 export** — writes your whole save as pretty JSON to `mirror-house-save.json` (a save dialog where the browser supports one, a plain download otherwise).
- **📂 import** — picks a save file back up. It is validated strictly — field by field, version checked — before anything is stored; a tampered or truncated file is rejected with a toast, never executed. The page then reloads onto the imported save.

A rejected or corrupt stored save reads as absent: the game boots fresh with sensible defaults rather than trusting junk.

### Install it — it works offline

Mirror House is a PWA. On the production build, use your browser's _Install app_ / _Add to Home Screen_ action: it gets its own window, its own icon (the gold lantern), and a service worker caches the shell and assets — installed once, it loads and plays with the network cut. The whole game ships as a single ~195 KB (gzipped) bundle, so the first load is quick and the offline copy is small.
