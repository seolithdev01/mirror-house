## 1. Beta announcement — Reddit r/WebGames (gamer audience)

**Title:** Mirror House — a free, open-source first-person mirror-maze escape (web + Android beta, no ads, no tracking)

**One-line pitch:**

> Descend a century-old palace of mirrors: frost the beacon-glass with your lantern's breath, outrun the shadow that walks your exact path, and reach the stair door before the house loses its temper.

**Body (pitch → hook → link → ask):**

Every floor is one 2–5 minute loop: the mirrors are the enemies' eyes, your
frost is the only attack, and the exit stays sealed until every beacon pane
is fogged. Ten floors make Tier 1 — each one deeper braids the maze tighter
and puts your own shadow closer behind you.

- **The mirrors fight back.** Echoes see you only through clear glass — fog a
  pane and you've closed a door in their face; fog enough around one and it
  stalls, spinning. Sprint into a mirror, though, and it cracks: seven cracks
  and the house collapses with you in it.
- **Nothing to install, nothing to pay, nothing leaking.** Runs in the browser
  (or install it as a PWA — it plays fully offline), the music is generated
  live in your browser, the save never leaves your device, and the whole thing
  is open source (Apache-2.0 / CC-BY 4.0).
- **Built for everyone it hunts.** Keyboard-only, mouse-only (one-handed), or
  hybrid; remap all 15 keys; captions for sound cues; reduce-flashing mode;
  text at 100/125/150%; first-person, four-pane, and 2D-map views — the map is
  a full way to play, not a minimap. The HUD speaks English and हिंदी.

Play it here: <https://mirror-house.seolith.com> — floors are sharable by seed
code, and there's a Daily everyone on Earth gets the same floor of.

**Feedback ask (append):**

> This is the 0.2.0 beta — Tier 1 (floors 1–10) is complete and the deeper
> tiers are coming. I'd love feedback on: how the difficulty ramps (floor 1 vs
> the Dwaar), which input mode you settled into, and anything that fights the
> accessibility settings instead of helping. Bugs → GitHub issues with your
> seed code; the layout reproduces itself: <https://github.com/seolithdev01/mirror-house/issues>

## 3. Venue-pilot pitch (local ventures)

For cafés, studios, bookshops, and other small spaces — email or DM shaped,
fill the bracketed holes, keep it short.

**Subject:** Your café, as a playable floor — free during the beta

> Hi [name] — I make [Mirror House](https://mirror-house.seolith.com), a free
> browser maze game. Local businesses can host a floor inside it, and I'm
> looking for [city] pilots while the beta runs.
>
> **What you get:** a themed floor of the game — your name on the banner, a
> one-line tagline you write (English and Hindi), your two brand colours on
> the exit and beacon glass, and the maze shaped to taste (calm and roomy, or
> tight and mean). Players reach it from Settings → Floors; a daily seed
> regenerates it every day, so there's always a reason to come back. It works
> on any phone browser — nothing to install — and the QR code at your counter
> is the whole funnel.
>
> **What it costs:** nothing during the beta — pilots are free, in exchange
> for telling me what players say. (After launch: a flat sponsor fee, never
> player-facing — no ads, no data, the game never sends anything anywhere.)
>
> **What it never touches:** each player's progress. A sponsored floor is a
> side excursion — it never alters their campaign, scores, or currency, and
> the banner says so up front.
>
> If you're curious, play the demo floor (⚙ Settings → Floors → Glasshouse
> Café) — that's exactly the shape of it. Want one for [venue name]?
>
> — [your name], [contact]

**The pack checklist** (what we need from the venue; the game validates every
field strictly — the demo pack
[`public/venues/demo-glasshouse-cafe.json`](../public/venues/demo-glasshouse-cafe.json)
is the worked example):

- [ ] **id** — slug, 3–24 chars of `a-z 0-9 -` (becomes `/venues/<id>.json`
      and the `?venue=` link behind the QR code)
- [ ] **name** — display name, ≤ 48 chars (the banner and the floor chip)
- [ ] **tagline** — one line, ≤ 160 chars, plus **locale.en / locale.hi**
      copies (each ≤ 160 chars — the Hindi one is shown to Hindi players)
- [ ] **accent.primary / accent.secondary** — two brand colours (hex →
      number), primary on the exit marker, secondary on the beacon glass
- [ ] **floor.width / height** — 11–25 each (the maze's footprint)
- [ ] **floor.braid** — 0–0.5 (how looped the maze is; ~0.15 calm, ~0.35 mean)
- [ ] **floor.beacons** — 1–6 (objectives per visit; 2 is a coffee-break floor)
- [ ] **floor.echoes** — 1–6 (the hunters; 3 is the demo's gentle ride)
- [ ] **floor.standing** — 0–10 (decorative standing mirrors — the selfie glass)
- [ ] sign-off: the venue approves the name/tagline text in both languages

## 4. Launch-day checklist

One pass, in order; every line names the artifact that proves it.

- [ ] **Web is live** — `npm run build` clean on `main`; Cloudflare Pages
      deployment of `0.2.0-beta.1` confirmed at
      <https://mirror-house.seolith.com> (CI deploys on push to `main`; the
      action log is the witness). Open the deployed build once and clear
      floor 1 by hand.
- [ ] **Version says beta** — `package.json` is `0.2.0-beta.1` and the
      Android `versionName` matches (`versionCode 1`) — see
      [BUILD-STORES.md](BUILD-STORES.md) for the bump rule.
- [ ] **Service worker turned** — `public/sw.js` CACHE is `mh-v5` and the
      precache list carries the demo venue pack; on the deployed build, load
      once online, then reload offline (airplane mode) and confirm the game
      AND ⚙ → Floors → demo floor still work.
- [ ] **Announcements out** — §1 (r/WebGames) and §2 (general audience)
      posted with the play link; reply to every comment for the first 48 h
      (feedback → GitHub issues, seeds in hand).
- [ ] **Stores (when ready)** — the Android build follows
      [BUILD-STORES.md](BUILD-STORES.md): `npm run cap:sync`, sign, upload
      the AAB; the listing copy to paste is [STORE-LISTING.md](STORE-LISTING.md).
      The debug + unsigned-release APKs are already build-proven (2026-11-09);
      what remains is the keystore, the Play account, and the upload.
      Stores are optional for launch day — the web build is the launch.
- [ ] **Venue pilots (the week after)** — send §3 to the first
      [3–5] venues; each new pack lands in `public/venues/` as its own JSON
      plus one line in the service worker precache (per-pack cache bump per
      BUILD-STORES), tested with `?venue=<id>`.
- [ ] **Triage loop warm** — issues template checked, seed codes reproducible
      (`?seed=CODE`), the playtest kit ([PLAYTEST.md](../PLAYTEST.md)) ready
      for anyone local.

- [ ] Replace the stock Capacitor **splash** PNGs (all densities, `android/app/src/main/res/drawable*/splash.png`) with the night-indigo lantern splash (`npm run icons` extension) before Play upload.
