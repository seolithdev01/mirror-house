# Mirror House — Accessibility

This document is written **from the shipped code** (milestone M5): every claim
below names the file and behaviour that implements it. The front door for all
of it is the ⚙ **Settings** overlay (`src/ui/settings.ts`, the ⚙ button in the
HUD, `src/ui/hud.ts`) — every control applies at once and persists in the
IndexedDB save (`src/game/save.ts`, `a11y` block).

## The four access modes

| Mode                         | Where        | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------- | ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Reduce flashing**          | ⚙ → Access   | Adds the `mh-reduced-flash` body class (`src/main.ts`): every overlay, banner and the caption bar fades in/out over 320 ms instead of appearing at once (`index.html`), and Taal's lantern holds one constant glow instead of pulsing (`beatGlow`, `src/game/taal.ts`). His stride keeps its rhythm — motion, not luminance.                                                                                   |
| **Captions for sound cues**  | ⚙ → Access   | Shows the house's sound cues as text: a bottom-centre bar (`#mh-captions`) carries the latest **two** lines, each for **4 s** (`CAPTION_TTL_MS`, `src/ui/captions.ts`). Repeated cues dedupe to one line per window. See the event list below.                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Text size**                | ⚙ → Display  | 100 / 125 / 150 % — one CSS variable, `--mh-text-scale`, that every HUD, overlay, chip and caption font multiplies in (`index.html`). Set on the body from the save at boot.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Controls (key remapping)** | ⚙ → Controls | All **15** actions rebindable (`DEFAULT_KEYMAP`, `src/game/save.ts`): click a row, press a key. A conflict guard refuses a key another action owns (only the default turn-right/push-door pair may share `E`, and reset-to-defaults is the one way back to it). Arrow keys and both Shift keys stay hardwired movement/sprint alternates under every remap, so the default experience can never be locked out. |

## One-handed play

**Mouse mode** (the 🖱 button, top right) is the one-handed scheme: hold the
**left** button to walk toward the cursor, hold **right** to sprint
(`mouseOnlyInput`, `src/engine/input.ts`). Every verb has a clickable HUD
button — ❄ Frost, ? FAQ, 💬 Aaina, ★ Daily, 🔊 mute, 💾/📂 save, 🌐 language,
⚙ settings — so the whole game loop (walk, sprint, frost beacons, buy hints,
clear floors) is playable with one hand on the mouse. The view cycle (`V`)
and the tactical pause (`T`) are the two keyboard-only conveniences; neither
is required to finish a floor.

## No quick-time events

Mirror House has **no QTEs**. There is no timed-prompt window anywhere in the
codebase: every verb (frost, step-through, push) is a deliberate key press or
click that fires the moment you make it. Timing pressure comes from the house
— Echoes sensing you through clear glass, your shadow walking your past,
Taal's beat — never from a flashing "press X now" prompt.

## Who's who in the dark — entity identity

The three entities that hunt or haunt you are distinguishable by
**silhouette + motion + caption**, never by colour alone. All three are
layer-0 figures: visible directly and inside every mirror.

| Entity                                            | Silhouette                                                                                                                                                      | Motion                                                                                                                                                                                           | Light                                                                                                        | Caption names it                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Echo** (`src/game/echoView.ts`)                 | A **translucent two-piece glass figure** — capsule body + sphere head, no arm, no lantern. Teal-white glass (`0x9fc4d4`) at 42 % opacity, metallic like a pane. | Patrols the floor's anchor loop at a constant walk (2.2 m/s); switches to flow-field pursuit when it senses you; **spins in place** when your frost blinds it.                                   | None — it is made of glass, not light.                                                                       | “The glass sees you — an Echo stirs” · “An Echo gives chase” · “An Echo wanders, blinded” |
| **Chhaya** (`createChhaya`, `src/game/avatar.ts`) | The **full Surveyor silhouette** — body, head, arm, lantern — in a solid near-black coat (`0x14141c`).                                                          | **Replays your exact recorded path**, seconds behind you (7 s on floor 1, tightening to 4 s by the Dwaar, `floorParams`, `src/game/campaign.ts`). She only ever walks where you walked.          | A steady **rose** lantern (`0x8a3a5c`).                                                                      | “Your shadow is {s}s behind” — once per attempt, with the live delay                      |
| **Taal** (`createTaal`, `src/game/avatar.ts`)     | The **full Surveyor silhouette** in solid obsidian (`0x1a1a22`). Floor 5 only.                                                                                  | **Metronomic lurch**: his stride scales with the 8-tick beat — a surge on the accent ticks, a crawl between them (`beatPulse`, `src/game/taal.ts`). The lurch never stops, even muted or paused. | A **gold** lantern (`0xd9a441`) that flares on the accent — and holds one steady glow under Reduce flashing. | “Taal's lantern flares — the house's temper rises” on his touch                           |

Your own reflection (`createAvatar`) wears the full silhouette with **your
chosen look** (default indigo-gold) and appears only inside mirrors — it hides
from the world on layer 1. The overhead map keeps the same distinctions
without colour reliance: you are the gold arrow, Chhaya a pale hollow ring,
Echo/radar pings fading red dots (`src/ui/overhead.ts`).

**Never colour alone:** an Echo is the only figure without an arm or lantern
(a shape read), Chhaya is the only figure that retraces your route (a motion
read), Taal is the only figure that lurches on the beat (a rhythm read) — and
the caption bar names each event in text.

## Reduced-flash audit summary

What was audited (M5/T1–T3) and what shipped:

- **Taal's lantern** — the one rhythmic light in the house. Under Reduce
  flashing it feeds a constant emissive (`BEAT_STATIC = 1.4`); without it, the
  glow pulses 2.2 / 0.3 on the 250 ms beat clock. The beat survives in his
  stride either way.
- **Beacon mirrors** — already pulse-free: static emissive intensities (0.25
  clear, 0.6 captured, `src/game/mirror.ts`). The fog/capture transitions are
  one-time material swaps, not rhythmic.
- **Overlays and banners** (FAQ, settings, tier, Aaina, cleared/descend/fail/
  hint/toast, caption bar) — under Reduce flashing every one fades over
  **320 ms** (≥ 300 ms); hidden overlays keep their layout box and go
  transparent + inert so opacity can transition where `display` cannot.
- **Overhead-map radar pings** _(ratified as-is)_ — a ping is a small red dot
  born at full opacity that then only **decays, linearly to zero over 4 s**
  (`pingOpacity`, `src/ui/overhead.ts`; `PING_TTL_MS`). It never strobes and
  never grows: Chhaya's cadence is one dot per 2 s, an Echo's sense ping at
  most one per 250 ms while it sees you, each a small-diameter dot on a dark
  canvas — not a large-area flash. Reviewed and deliberately left unchanged;
  revisiting it means a fade-in curve in `pingOpacity`, nothing else.
- **The caption bar itself** — joins the same fade family: lines appear and
  leave over 320 ms under Reduce flashing, instantly otherwise. Text never
  blinks or scrolls.

## What the captions say

Every event below lands as one line (latest two on screen, 4 s each, en/hi
with the HUD language — `src/ui/i18n.ts`, `caption.*` keys):

- An Echo's sense check succeeds — “The glass sees you — an Echo stirs”
- An Echo starts a chase — “An Echo gives chase”
- An Echo is blinded by frost — “An Echo wanders, blinded”
- Chhaya's first appearance on the attempt — “Your shadow is {s}s behind”
- Taal's touch — “Taal's lantern flares — the house's temper rises”
- House anger crossing 60 — “The house is angry — the mirrors hum”
- House anger crossing 90 — “The house is furious — the mirrors tremble”
- A fail (caught / collapse), a clear (with your time), the descent banner,
  the tier-complete claim
- A hint purchased from Aaina — “Aaina's hint is yours”
- The music starting — “The music begins” — and muting/unmuting
