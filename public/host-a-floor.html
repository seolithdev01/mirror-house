Your venue, as a playable floor

Mirror House is a free, open-source browser maze game — a hundred floors of mirrors, and the things that hunt you through them. Local ventures can host a floor inside it: a themed side-floor with your name on the door.

Ask about hosting a floor Play the demo floor

What you get

  • Your name on the banner — shown the whole time a player is on your floor.
  • A one-line tagline you write, in English and Hindi.
  • Your two brand colours on the exit and the beacon glass.
  • A maze shaped to taste — calm and roomy, or tight and mean.
  • A fresh floor every day — a daily seed regenerates the maze, so regulars have a reason to come back, and everyone at your tables plays the same one.
  • A link and a QR code for your counter — it runs in any phone browser. Nothing to install, no account, no sign-up.

What it costs

Nothing during the beta. Pilot floors are free, in exchange for telling us what your customers say about it.

After launch a hosted floor is a flat sponsor fee, agreed up front — never per-click, never per-player, and never anything a player pays.

What it never does

  • No ads, no tracking, no data. The game sends nothing anywhere — there is no analytics to sell you, and none collected about your customers.
  • It never touches a player's progress. A hosted floor is a side excursion: it does not change their campaign, scores or currency, and the banner says so.
  • Your brand stays yours. Your name and colours live in your floor's own small pack file, used only there.

How it works

  1. Play the Glasshouse Café demo floor — that is exactly the shape of it.
  2. Send the short enquiry: venue name, tagline, two colours, the mood of the maze.
  3. We build your pack, send you a private link to try, and publish it when you are happy.
